A Pokemon Shiny hunter made entirely from scratch!

The Rig

This is my Pokemon Shiny Hunter which is designed to hunt Shiny Pokemon on the Nintendo Switch or the Switch 2!

It is able to shiny hunt tons of Pokemon from Sword, with the ability to shiny hunt specific ones from Brilliant Diamond!

The Flow

The Raspberry Pi Controls the Switch using NXBT, a Bluetooth Controller Emulator.

The Shiny Hunter then loads up the screen where the Shiny Pokemon will be.

It will then look for a menu item that appears faster for Non-Shiny Pokemon.

Repeat until you get your Shiny Pokemon! It will automatically reset if no Shiny is detected.

The Struggles

By far the biggest issue with this project was the unreliability of NXBT itself. Because it has to go through Bluetooth, many inputs were being dropped.

To fix this, I put in a series of checks. If any of the below checks fail, it will recover and keep on crusing.
