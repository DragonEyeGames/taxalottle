# The Layout
I styled the main page to look like the Ocean! Going with this theme, I made the individual project pages styled after the shore! I feel like this is a great way to display my work and it fits in well with my mascot.
I used **JavaScript** to animate the cool waves that flow on the pages. I think that it turned out rather nice even though it was rather difficult to setup.
I also went with a card layout with lots of pictures to make it visually easy to read as it is broken up.
I personally think that this color scheme is nice and fun on the project pages, making you want to stay there longer!
The layout was made with CSS for styling and HTML for layout, just like a lot of websites out there. Then there was the previously mentioned JavaScript for animating the wave.

# The Waves
These were by far the most challenging part of the project.
The wave on the ocean was relatively simple, while the wave on the shore was difficult. It actually consists of 6 different SVGs.
On the shore there are two sets of waves, each consisting of three svgs. One SVG is the water, another is slightly offset and is the semi-transparent white tip, and a third is the receding water line. This is the spot that turns dark after the water passes over it and fades after it recedes.
These waves are animated with a variety of Sin waves in JavaScript, making them flow naturally like the ocean. I think it ended up being a pretty convincing wave and I am rather pleased with the final results.
One thing I like is how simplistic it looks. I am a big fan of 'cartoony' style stuff. I like very few colors and outlines where it makes sense. I think I did a good job accomplishing that on these waves and my site as a whole.
